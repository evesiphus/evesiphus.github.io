## Research Areas

- **AI/ML for High-Speed Softwarized Networks** (2022–Present): Non-intrusive performance intelligence and drift-resilient MLOps for NFV data planes.
- **Large-Scale Modeling of NGMA Networks** (2024–Present): Stochastic modeling of uplink RSMA, NOMA, and OMA with discrete rate adaptation.
- **Spatiotemporal Modeling and Learning for Dynamic Networks**: Stochastic geometry, queueing theory, and reinforcement learning for network stability.
